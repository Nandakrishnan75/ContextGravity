Antigravity Continuity Architecture & Guide

Why did my conversations disappear from the IDE?

When Google Antigravity restarts or closes, it unloads past sessions from active memory to keep the interface fast. Your conversations, code edits, and artifacts are never deleted. They are stored at:

Windows: %USERPROFILE%\.gemini\antigravity-ide\brain\<id>\.system_generated\logs\transcript.jsonl
Linux: ~/.gemini/antigravity-ide/brain/<id>/.system_generated/logs/transcript.jsonl

How to restore continuity in 3 steps:

  1. Find your previous conversation in this dashboard.
  2. Click "Copy Continuity Prompt".
  3. Open a new chat in Antigravity and paste the prompt. Antigravity uses its built-in file inspection tools to read its own local transcript and artifacts, regaining full memory instantly.

Running on Windows and Linux:

  • Windows: Double click start.bat
  • Linux / macOS: Run ./start.sh or node server.js
